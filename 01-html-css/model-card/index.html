Tabular Credit Risk Predictor

A tabular credit risk predictor is a machine learning classifier trained on historical financial data—such as income, debt-to-income ratio, credit history, employment length, and loan amount—to predict whether a loan applicant is likely to default. Enterprise finance teams use these models to automate underwriting decisions while managing credit exposure. From an engineering and ethics standpoint, a model card for this use case must explicitly document feature importance, demographic parity or fairness metrics across applicant subgroups, and the severe financial impact of false positives versus false negatives.
